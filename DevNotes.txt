Steps to deploy to RPi Pico
1. Hold left button and press right button
2. Mount the USB drive that appeared
3. Copy the UF2 file to the drive
4. Pico will automatically apply the firmware update and restart
5. lsusb to get list of attached devices
    ex. Bus 005 Device 032: ID 0a7b:d000
6. lsusb -v -s BUS:DEV to get list
    ex. lsusb -v -s 005:032 # device 32 on bus 5
